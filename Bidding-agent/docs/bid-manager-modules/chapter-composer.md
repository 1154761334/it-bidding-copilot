# chapter-composer

## Role

Expand approved chapter work files into internal draft chapters.

## Inputs

- outline placeholder
- chapter work files
- mapping sheet
- evidence retrieval sheet

## Outputs

- internal chapter drafts
- chapter-level missing-material notes
- chapter-level pending verification items

## Hard rules

- do not bypass chapter work files
- do not emit formal delivery wording when evidence is still pending
- preserve ownership boundaries in every chapter
